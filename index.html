P+L 4 — Unidentified Specimens

Note: Please do not reverse image search. These are famous specimens and you will find them immediately. The accuracy of your tree will not affect your grade. Your grade will be based on the level of effort: details and well-described logic, integration of class discussions and reading, obvious effort during class. This assignment is about experimenting with different hypotheses and describing your process.

The specimen number is S# or M#. Mandibles are jaw bones. The “cc” number indicates the cranial capacity, or size of the brain. The specimens don’t have any other information on purpose. You’re not supposed to know the species!
